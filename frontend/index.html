How it works

Three quick steps to get automatic opponent prep while you play

1

Toggle Listening

On your Dashboard, switch on Listening before your session. The app will watch your Lichess games in real time.

2

Play on Lichess

Just play as usual. When a game starts, we detect your opponent and prepare targeted insights.

3

Review the Prep

View opponent analysis in your Dashboard and the Public Table. Use it to guide your opening choices.
